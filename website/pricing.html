Frequently asked questions

Is anything missing from the self-hosted version?

No. Self-hosting includes every feature, and always will, including the ones a hosted tier leaves out. With a hosted plan, we take care of backups, updates and monitoring for you.

The free version is licensed for noncommercial use: your own household, study, research, a hobby project, and charities, schools and public bodies. Using it to run a business (including keeping the books of a company or a freelance practice) needs either a hosted plan or a commercial licence, which you can ask for at hello@flaxvin.tech.

How does Gmail work on a hosted plan?

Every plan, Solo included, can fetch bank alerts and statements from Gmail once you bring your own Google keys. You make a free OAuth client in your own Google Cloud project (about ten minutes, step by step), paste its client ID and secret into Settings, and connect. You consent to your own application, not ours; your instance only runs it.

One set of keys can serve the whole household, or each member can use their own. The secret is encrypted before it is stored and never shown again. We don't ask Google for access to your inbox under our own name, because that needs an annual third-party security audit for any public service, and its cost would be added to every plan, including the ones where nobody uses Gmail.

Where does my data actually live?

Self-hosted, it lives wherever you put it.

On a hosted plan, in its own database: one container and one file per customer, not a shared table with your row in it. The provider is Fly.io and the default region is Singapore, which is the closest one to India that Fly operates. Fly retired its Mumbai region, so nothing here is hosted inside India. Another region is available on request; it may carry a different rate, and we will tell you what before anything is deployed.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

It is deleted five days after the plan ends, whether you cancelled, let it expire, or a payment failed. The live database, every backup and every attachment go together. There is no archive, no grace copy, and no way for us to bring it back afterwards, however soon you ask.

Five days is short on purpose. Keeping your financial records after you have stopped paying for the service is a risk, to you more than to us.

Please export first. One endpoint returns everything in an open format, at any time, on every plan, and the result is a SQLite file you can open with anything. The free version will load it straight back in.

Can I get my data out?

One endpoint returns everything in an open format, any time, on every plan. The database is a SQLite file you can open with anything. Cancelling gets you the file.

This is deliberate, and it is tested: the test suite checks that the export is complete.

What is “on demand”, and should I pay to avoid it?

Every hosted plan runs your household in its own container with its own database file. Both prices get the same software. The only difference is whether that container is allowed to stop.

On demand stops your container after a spell with nobody using it, and starts it again on your next visit. That takes a second or two, about as long as a page load on a slow connection. Once awake, it stays awake while you are using it. Most households open their budget a few times a day, so it is asleep most of the month, which is what keeps this price low.

Always on never stops. Every page is instant, and a script calling the API never waits for a wake-up. It's a good choice if you check the budget many times a day, or if you automate against it.

Nothing is lost while it sleeps. Your database is a file on a volume that stays exactly where it is, and scheduled work (the nightly backup, the digest, price refresh) runs on both, whether or not anyone is using the app.

You can switch between the two at any time, and the change takes effect on your next bill.

Is there a free trial?

There is a live demo with three years of data you can change however you like, and a free version you can run forever. Between them, you can find out whether Pathayam suits you before paying anything.

That is also why there are no refunds: fees already paid are not returned, and cancelling stops the next charge rather than reversing the last one. Every paid feature can be tried first.

What happens if you stop running the service?

The source is public and the data format is documented, so at worst you move the file to your own machine and carry on. That is why the free tier stays unlimited: it means the hosted tier is safe to buy.
