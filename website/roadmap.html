Roadmap

What comes next, and when.

This is what Flaxvin Technologies plans to build next for Pathayam, in order. The dates are targets and may move as the work progresses.

How to read this

Everything below is a plan rather than a commitment. Nothing here is sold or pre-paid, and an item can move or be dropped if it turns out to be the wrong thing to build.

The order is deliberate. Smaller, lower-risk work comes first. Anything that touches the accounting engine, which guarantees that your accounts, envelopes and Ready to Assign always reconcile to the rupee, is handled with the most care, because that guarantee is central to Pathayam.

Two items near the bottom depend on decisions that are not about engineering. They are listed as longshots and may never happen, and we would rather say so openly.

Recently shipped

What we have released in recent months.

  • Gmail on every plan: bring a Google OAuth client from your own Google Cloud project, for yourself or for the whole household, and fetch bank alerts and statements on Solo, Household or Power. The secret is encrypted at rest. How to make one. Nov 2026
  • Ask an AI about your money: connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini (the app where Google offers custom apps, or Gemini CLI) or any MCP app to a server built into Pathayam, and ask about your own figures. Read-only unless you allow it to file and annotate transactions and suggest new ones for you to approve in Review, where the operator turns that on. It sees what you see and no more, masks account numbers, UPI IDs, phone numbers and PAN by default, and every question it asked is listed. How it works. Nov 2026
  • Digital gold from SafeGold: SafeGold’s monthly statement, uploaded or fetched from Gmail, records its buys as lots with GST in the cost and its sells as realised gains, checked against the closing gold balance. Nov 2026
  • Employer NPS in the tax estimate: the employer’s contribution under 80CCD(2) lowers the estimate under both regimes, capped at its share of basic + DA. Nov 2026
  • Card statements that ask for the card’s digits: a statement locked with digits the card’s account lacks asks for them on import and keeps them; after a Gmail fetch each such card is listed with a box. A card keeps up to its last eight digits, and SBI Card and Canara card statements read their credits, debits and narrations right. Nov 2026
  • Password links: in a household that signs in with passwords, a member makes a one-time link that sets a newcomer’s first password; whoever runs the server can make one with npm run password-link. Oct 2026
  • Deposits that value themselves: give a fixed or recurring deposit, a PPF or an EPF its terms, and it is worth what the arithmetic says on any day. The maturity shows on the account page, the cashflow calendar and the Overview. Oct 2026
  • Adding with no signal: the Add form keeps an entry on the device and sends it when the connection is back. The Add form only, and nothing is read offline. Oct 2026
  • Tax from the ledger: home-loan interest under 24(b) from the loans themselves, TDS and tax already paid netted to a balance or a refund, and each advance-tax instalment checked against what was paid by its date. Oct 2026
  • Insurance register: policies, premiums and renewals, with the 80C and 80D premiums offered to the tax estimate. Oct 2026
  • Printing: the tax estimate, reports, month close, net worth and a loan's schedule print as documents. Oct 2026
  • Import from YNAB or Actual Budget: their CSV exports land in Review with transfers and splits intact. Oct 2026
  • GST on card EMIs: 18% on each instalment's interest, as its own card charge. Oct 2026
  • Listed bonds and 2024-25 gains: a listed bond held over a year is taxed at 12.5%, 2024-25 gains are taxed at the rates of their sale date either side of 23-07-2024, and a year with no rates is refused. Oct 2026

Planned

In order. Each phase starts when the one before it is released, so an early delay moves everything after it. That is why the later date ranges are wider.

  1. Locked envelopes Q4 2026

    Mark an envelope so its money cannot be moved out, such as school fees, the insurance sinking fund, or anything that must survive a tight month. Adding to a locked envelope always works; only taking from it is refused, and spending you actually did is still recorded.

    Roughly 4–6 weeks.

  2. Targets on a whole group Q4 2026 – Q1 2027

    Set one target across a group of envelopes rather than on each, and see the group’s own subtotal on the budget screen. Useful when you care that “Food” gets ₹18,000 and not how it divides between groceries, vegetables and eating out.

    Roughly 10–16 weeks for both halves.

  3. Spending in another currency Q2 2027

    Enter a transaction as $40 and have it stored as the rupees it cost. Your envelopes stay in rupees, and the conversion happens as you type, outside the budget.

    On a card used abroad, the real rupee figure is your bank’s, markup and cross-currency fee included, which is typically a few percent away from a market rate. So the converted amount is marked an estimate until your statement arrives, and then corrected to what you were actually charged.

    Roughly 8–12 weeks.

  4. Card milestones Q2 – Q4 2027

    Track spend against the thresholds your cards actually use: per statement cycle, per financial-year quarter, and per financial year, so you don’t miss an annual fee waiver or a quarterly voucher.

    It will say plainly that it is an estimate. Bank statements and SMS do not carry the merchant category reliably, so the rules work off your envelopes and payees, which is close but not the issuer’s own arithmetic.

    Roughly 14–20 weeks.

  5. Logging by chat Q4 2027 – Q1 2028

    Send “450 groceries hdfc” to a Telegram bot and have it land in your budget, with a confirmation before anything is written. Telegram first because it needs nothing but a bot token and works on a home server behind a normal router.

    Roughly 10–16 weeks.

Longshots

We would like to build both, and the work itself is not the obstacle. Each depends on a question that has nothing to do with code, and they may never happen.

Account Aggregator sync No date

Pulling transactions automatically through the RBI’s Account Aggregator framework requires being a registered Financial Information User, an entity-level registration with an aggregator, not something an individual household can hold. It could only work for instances we host, and only if your financial data passed through us on the way to you.

That runs against how Pathayam is designed, so we would never do it quietly. If it ever happens, it will be a separate, opt-in choice, explained in full before you make it, and the answer may well stay no.

WhatsApp logging No date

This raises the same question. WhatsApp requires a publicly reachable webhook, which a self-hosted instance behind a home router does not have, so it would mean your messages passing through a relay we operate. Telegram achieves the same thing without that, which is why Telegram is on the plan above and this is not.

Considered and set aside

Ideas we have considered and decided against for now, with our reasons.

  • Envelopes that hold foreign currency. Different from entering a transaction in another currency, which is planned above. An envelope that genuinely holds dollars either breaks the guarantee that everything reconciles, or splits Ready to Assign into one figure per currency. Envelopes stay in rupees only, by design.
  • Envelopes nested inside envelopes. Group subtotals are planned; envelopes that fund each other are not. A parent holding money while its children also hold money is an easy way to count the same rupee twice.
  • Reward points arithmetic. Milestones are planned; points accrual is not. Rates, accelerated categories and monthly caps change often enough that the numbers would go quietly stale, which is worse than not showing them.
