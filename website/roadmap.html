What comes next, and when.

This is what Flaxvin Technologies plans to build next for Pathayam, in order. The dates are targets and may move as the work progresses.

Planned Recently shipped

Last reviewed 14 November 2026.

How to read this

Everything below is a plan rather than a commitment. Nothing here is sold or pre-paid, and an item can move or be dropped if it turns out to be the wrong thing to build.

The order is deliberate. Smaller, lower-risk work comes first. Anything that touches the accounting engine, which guarantees that your accounts, envelopes and Ready to Assign always reconcile to the rupee, is handled with the most care, because that guarantee is central to Pathayam.

Two items near the bottom depend on decisions that are not about engineering. They are listed as longshots and may never happen, and we would rather say so openly.

Recently shipped

What we have released in recent months.

  • Nov 2026 A tax estimate that reads your AIS more closely

    With your AIS in, the standard deduction goes on your salary alone, not on interest and dividends, and the old regime deducts 80TTA, or 80TTB once you are 60, from the interest it reports. How it works.

  • Nov 2026 Prices every night, and spending that counts your cards

    Every price is refreshed at midnight IST, and a share that has moved to a new ISIN after a split is flagged rather than mispriced. Reports now count card purchases as spending, as the Overview does, and a card EMI’s processing fee shows in its total cost.

  • Nov 2026 Tax-free long-term gains, before 31 March

    The tax estimate shows how much of your ₹1.25 lakh long-term equity exemption is still unused this year, and how many units of each holding could be sold, oldest lots first, to use it without going over. An estimate at the last stored price, with exit loads and charges left out; whether to sell, and whether to buy back, stays your call.

  • Nov 2026 Your Annual Information Statement, read for you

    Upload your AIS on the Tax page and we read the TDS, interest, dividends and salary the department already has on record, show you securities sold beside what you recorded, and offer the figures to your estimate for you to check and save. We keep the figures, not the file, and only you see them.

  • Nov 2026 Shares and ETFs priced for you

    Give an Indian share or ETF its ISIN or NSE symbol and it takes each evening’s closing price from NSE’s and BSE’s own published files. No API key, no account, and nothing about what you hold is sent; just the day’s file is fetched.

  • Nov 2026 Private loans that stay private

    A loan marked private now keeps its payment envelope in its holder’s own budget, so its name, its EMI and every instalment stay off the household budget, Reports and Activity. Whose loan it is.

  • Nov 2026 A careful pass through every screen

    31 fixes from a review of the whole app, top to bottom. Reconciling a card or loan asks how much you owe; a move larger than an envelope holds needs you to allow it below zero; a goal’s monthly figure counts what is already in this month; there is no independence target until 30 days of history; and statement PDFs are read on their own with a time limit, so one heavy file no longer holds up the app.

  • Nov 2026 Gmail on every plan

    Bring a Google OAuth client from your own Google Cloud project, for yourself or for the whole household, and fetch bank alerts and statements on Solo, Household or Power. The secret is encrypted at rest. How to make one.

  • Nov 2026 Ask an AI about your money

    Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini (the app where Google offers custom apps, or Gemini CLI) or any MCP app to a server built into Pathayam, and ask about your own figures. Read-only unless you allow it to file and annotate transactions and suggest new ones for you to approve in Review, where the operator turns that on. It sees what you see and no more, masks account numbers, UPI IDs, phone numbers and PAN by default, and every question it asked is listed. How it works.

  • Nov 2026 Digital gold from SafeGold

    SafeGold’s monthly statement, uploaded or fetched from Gmail, records its buys as lots with GST in the cost and its sells as realised gains, checked against the closing gold balance.

  • Nov 2026 Employer NPS in the tax estimate

    The employer’s contribution under 80CCD(2) lowers the estimate under both regimes, capped at its share of basic + DA.

  • Nov 2026 Card statements that ask for the card’s digits

    A statement locked with digits the card’s account lacks asks for them on import and keeps them; after a Gmail fetch each such card is listed with a box. A card keeps up to its last eight digits, and SBI Card and Canara card statements read their credits, debits and narrations right.

  • Oct 2026 Password links

    In a household that signs in with passwords, a member makes a one-time link that sets a newcomer’s first password; whoever runs the server can make one with npm run password-link.

  • Oct 2026 Deposits that value themselves

    Give a fixed or recurring deposit, a PPF or an EPF its terms, and it is worth what the arithmetic says on any day. The maturity shows on the account page, the cashflow calendar and the Overview.

  • Oct 2026 Adding with no signal

    The Add form keeps an entry on the device and sends it when the connection is back. The Add form only, and nothing is read offline.

  • Oct 2026 Tax from the ledger

    Home-loan interest under 24(b) from the loans themselves, TDS and tax already paid netted to a balance or a refund, and each advance-tax instalment checked against what was paid by its date.

  • Oct 2026 Insurance register

    Policies, premiums and renewals, with the 80C and 80D premiums offered to the tax estimate.

  • Oct 2026 Printing

    The tax estimate, reports, month close, net worth and a loan’s schedule print as documents.

  • Oct 2026 Import from YNAB or Actual Budget

    Their CSV exports land in Review with transfers and splits intact.

  • Oct 2026 GST on card EMIs

    18% on each instalment’s interest, as its own card charge.

  • Oct 2026 Listed bonds and 2024-25 gains

    A listed bond held over a year is taxed at 12.5%, 2024-25 gains are taxed at the rates of their sale date either side of 23-07-2024, and a year with no rates is refused.

  • Oct 2026 Net worth that adds up

    Money owed to family and other liabilities are counted, and the parts of the change add up to the total.

  • Oct 2026 More statements open themselves

    Axis and HDFC account statements from the customer ID, HSBC card statements from the card’s last six, and Standard Chartered statements recognised.

  • Sep 2026 Schedules on a weekday

    “the first Sunday of each month”, “the last Friday”, alongside the existing date rules.

  • Sep 2026 Envelope lines everywhere

    One control for splitting across envelopes, on adding and editing both transactions and schedules. The first line takes whatever the others leave, so the lines can never disagree with the total.

  • Sep 2026 Capital gains in the tax estimate

    Sorted by asset class and holding period, taxed at their own rates rather than slab rates.

  • Sep 2026 Sign in without Google

    A password, or any OpenID Connect provider you already run.

Planned

In order. Each phase starts when the one before it is released, so an early delay moves everything after it. That is why the later date ranges are wider.

  1. Q4 2026

    Roughly 4 to 6 weeks.

    Locked envelopes

    Mark an envelope so its money cannot be moved out, such as school fees, the insurance sinking fund, or anything that must survive a tight month. Adding to a locked envelope always works; only taking from it is refused, and spending you actually did is still recorded.

  2. Q4 2026 to Q1 2027

    Roughly 10 to 16 weeks for both halves.

    Targets on a whole group

    Set one target across a group of envelopes rather than on each, and see the group’s own subtotal on the budget screen. Useful when you care that “Food” gets ₹18,000 and not how it divides between groceries, vegetables and eating out.

  3. Q2 2027

    Roughly 8 to 12 weeks.

    Spending in another currency

    Enter a transaction as $40 and have it stored as the rupees it cost. Your envelopes stay in rupees, and the conversion happens as you type, outside the budget.

    On a card used abroad, the real rupee figure is your bank’s, markup and cross-currency fee included, which is typically a few percent away from a market rate. So the converted amount is marked an estimate until your statement arrives, and then corrected to what you were actually charged.

  4. Q2 to Q4 2027

    Roughly 14 to 20 weeks.

    Card milestones

    Track spend against the thresholds your cards actually use: per statement cycle, per financial-year quarter, and per financial year, so you don’t miss an annual fee waiver or a quarterly voucher.

    It will say plainly that it is an estimate. Bank statements and SMS do not carry the merchant category reliably, so the rules work off your envelopes and payees, which is close but not the issuer’s own arithmetic.

  5. Q4 2027 to Q1 2028

    Roughly 10 to 16 weeks.

    Logging by chat

    Send “450 groceries hdfc” to a Telegram bot and have it land in your budget, with a confirmation before anything is written. Telegram first because it needs nothing but a bot token and works on a home server behind a normal router.

Longshots

We would like to build both, and the work itself is not the obstacle. Each depends on a question that has nothing to do with code, and they may never happen.

  • No date

    Account Aggregator sync

    Pulling transactions automatically through the RBI’s Account Aggregator framework requires being a registered Financial Information User, an entity-level registration with an aggregator, not something an individual household can hold. It could only work for instances we host, and only if your financial data passed through us on the way to you.

    That runs against how Pathayam is designed, so we would never do it quietly. If it ever happens, it will be a separate, opt-in choice, explained in full before you make it, and the answer may well stay no.

  • No date

    WhatsApp logging

    This raises the same question. WhatsApp requires a publicly reachable webhook, which a self-hosted instance behind a home router does not have, so it would mean your messages passing through a relay we operate. Telegram achieves the same thing without that, which is why Telegram is on the plan above and this is not.

Considered and set aside

Ideas we have considered and decided against for now, with our reasons.

Envelopes that hold foreign currency.
Different from entering a transaction in another currency, which is planned above. An envelope that genuinely holds dollars either breaks the guarantee that everything reconciles, or splits Ready to Assign into one figure per currency. Envelopes stay in rupees only, by design.
Envelopes nested inside envelopes.
Group subtotals are planned; envelopes that fund each other are not. A parent holding money while its children also hold money is an easy way to count the same rupee twice.
Reward points arithmetic.
Milestones are planned; points accrual is not. Rates, accelerated categories and monthly caps change often enough that the numbers would go quietly stale, which is worse than not showing them.
