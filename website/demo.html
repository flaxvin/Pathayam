Three rules the demo is enforcing

The same three the app enforces on real money.

You can only budget money you have

Ready to Assign starts at what actually arrived. Assign more and it goes negative, and the app will not let that stand — a plan built on money you do not have is not a plan.

Overspending is never hidden

Push an envelope below zero and it turns red and stays red. You choose how it resolves: cover it from elsewhere now, or let the rollover reduce next month.

Order is information

Funding every target spends Ready to Assign down the budget in the order you arranged it, so what matters most is funded first and any shortfall lands where you will see it.

What a nine-row demo leaves out

The real thing carries the parts that make this survivable past month three.

  • Cards. Spending on one reserves the cash to clear it, the moment the charge lands.
  • Rollover. Next month opens with every leftover already in place.
  • Money arriving on its own. Statements, CSVs and alerts become a review queue.
  • Undo. Every action here would be reversible for thirty days.
