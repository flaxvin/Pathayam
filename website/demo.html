What just happened

The three rules the demo is enforcing

R1

You can only budget money you have

Ready to Assign starts at what actually arrived. Assign more than that and it goes negative — the app will not let that stand, because a plan built on money you do not have is not a plan.

R4

Overspending is never hidden

Push an envelope below zero and it turns red and stays red. You choose how it resolves: cover it from another envelope now, or let the rollover reduce next month's Ready to Assign. Both models ship.

R9

Order is information

Fund every target spends Ready to Assign down the budget in the order you arranged it, so what matters most is funded first and the shortfall lands at the bottom where you can see it.

The difference in the real thing

What a seven-row demo leaves out

  • Credit cards. Spending on a card moves money into a payment envelope, so the cash to clear the bill is reserved the moment the charge lands.
  • Rollover. Next month opens with every envelope's leftover already in it, and overspending handled under whichever model you chose.
  • Money arriving on its own. Statement PDFs, CSVs and bank alerts become review rows; nothing posts without a person.
  • Undo. Every action above would be reversible for thirty days, from an append-only log.
