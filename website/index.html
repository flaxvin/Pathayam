How it works Set it up

Self-hosted, and on the hosted Household and Power plans. Not on Solo.

What an AI reads is sent to that AI’s provider. If you don’t connect one, nothing is sent anywhere.

Read-only unless you say so

By default an AI can look at the budget and change nothing. Allow one app to make changes and it can file and split transactions, and suggest new ones for you to approve. It can never delete anything or change a setting, and every change can be undone.

It sees what you see

Each connection reads as the member who approved it: the household budget and your own. Another member’s private accounts stay out, exactly as they do on every screen.

Masked, and logged

Account numbers, UPI IDs, phone numbers and PAN are masked before anything is sent, unless you turn it off. Every question an AI asked and every change it made is listed, and one click disconnects it.

Give every rupee a job

When income arrives, you assign it: rent, groceries, the card bill, the yearly payment in March that is easy to forget. When Ready to Assign reaches zero, your month is planned.

  • Rollover carries every leftover, and every overspend, into next month
  • Overspending is always shown. Cover it now, or let it reduce next month
  • Targets, and one tap to fund all of them in the order you arranged

Your cards, in the order they fall due

Spending on a card moves money into its payment envelope straight away, so the cash to clear the bill is already set aside. One screen ranks every card by what is due next.

  • What is owed, what is set aside, and what has nothing behind it
  • Statement amount, date, due date and minimum once you record one
  • A shortfall is never reported larger than the card actually owes

Bring your transactions in without retyping

Statement PDFs from twelve Indian banks, opened in-process even when they are password-protected. Also CSVs, bank alerts, a consolidated statement for your holdings, SafeGold statements for digital gold, and exports from YNAB and Actual Budget if you are switching over.

  • Five dedupe tiers, so one purchase never lands twice
  • Every parse checked against the statement's own closing balance
  • File a payee twice and a rule is proposed, never applied without your approval

Know what the whole picture is worth

Holdings are tracked as units with FIFO lots, so cost basis and XIRR are exact rather than estimated. Net worth is split into what you saved, what the market and the exchange rate did, and what you paid off (including what you owe family), and the parts add up to the total change.

  • Mutual funds and currency rates refresh themselves; every price carries its date
  • Fixed and recurring deposits, PPF and EPF value themselves from their terms; gold and anything else hand-valued is updated in one sitting
  • None of it appears on the budget screen, because growth is not income

Three things it will not do

Some of our commitments are about what Pathayam leaves out.

Ask you to trust anybody

No telemetry, no analytics and no third parties, even for sign-in: a password is built in and Google is optional. Self-host, and nothing leaves your machine unless you connect an AI yourself. Host with us, and your data has its own database, never a row in a shared table.

Post anything behind your back

Every imported row waits for your review, with its raw text kept beside it. Rules are proposed, never applied silently, and each proposal shows what it was based on.

Make it hard to leave

The database is one SQLite file you can open with anything. The export is complete and documented. Nothing about the format needs this app to read it.
